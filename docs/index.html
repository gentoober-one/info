Who am I?

Felipe Fernandes (a.k.a. Gentoober) | Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | Cybersecurity & Linux Engineering

I'm a cybersecurity professional with a strong background in Linux systems, IT infrastructure, networking, and security engineering. I currently serve as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) at Silvercore Services.

My work sits at the intersection of offensive security, defensive engineering, infrastructure architecture, and information security governance.

I believe effective security requires more than deploying tools or following checklists. It requires understanding how systems actually work, identifying where trust boundaries fail, and translating technical findings into practical, measurable improvements.

What I Do

Security Leadership — Silvercore Services

As CISO at Silvercore, my focus is on aligning cybersecurity strategy with business objectives, strengthening risk management practices, and integrating security into the company's technical operations.

Silvercore operates across cybersecurity, web development, software engineering, automation, and AI-powered solutions. My role involves addressing the security challenges associated with these areas, from infrastructure and application security to access control, secure development practices, and operational resilience.

I approach security leadership with a hands-on engineering mindset. Technical understanding and executive decision-making should reinforce each other, not exist in isolation.

Security Assessments & Engineering — EQS Engenharia

Previously, I worked as an Information Security Analyst at EQS Engenharia, conducting authorized security assessments across corporate networks, infrastructure services, web applications, and APIs.

My work included identifying and validating security weaknesses related to network segmentation, exposed services, access control, authentication, session management, and insecure configurations.

I performed authorized internal penetration tests, penetration tests of external, internet-facing applications, and vulnerability assessments, combining automated reconnaissance with manual verification to distinguish confirmed findings from potential risks.

Beyond vulnerability discovery, I produced technical and executive reports containing evidence, risk classifications, business impact assessments, prioritized remediation recommendations, and validation criteria.

I also developed infrastructure modernization proposals addressing network architecture, segmentation, secure remote access, centralized administration, identity management, and operational security.

This experience reinforced a principle I consider essential: finding a vulnerability is only part of the job; communicating its impact and defining a realistic path to remediation is equally important.

Technical Background

Linux & Infrastructure Engineering

Linux has been a central part of my technical background for years, particularly Gentoo Linux and OpenRC.

My interests extend well beyond everyday system administration. I enjoy understanding operating systems from boot and kernel initialization to userspace, networking, filesystems, and application behavior.

My cybersecurity work combines an offensive perspective with defensive engineering.

  • Defensive Security: System hardening, access control, network segmentation, logging, monitoring, vulnerability management, and remediation planning.
